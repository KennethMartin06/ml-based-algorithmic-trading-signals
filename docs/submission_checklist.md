## Results summary (honest negative result)

* No ML model predicted next-day direction reliably better than the majority-class baseline (14 of 15
  bootstrap intervals for the accuracy difference include zero; one is significantly worse).
* 4 of 15 ML trading strategies had a higher test Sharpe ratio than buy-and-hold. None of these differences is
  statistically reliable, and 5 strategies were identical to buy-and-hold.
* Walk-forward 2016–2025: 0 of 15 ML strategies beat buy-and-hold on Sharpe ratio or cumulative return.
