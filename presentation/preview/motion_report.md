## What this does and does not prove

- The read-back and XML checks prove which transitions and effects are configured, on which objects, in what order and with what timing.
- The video export proves that PowerPoint itself plays them: Morph slides change over many frames rather than cutting.
- Neither replaces a rehearsal on the presentation machine. Morph needs PowerPoint 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365. Older versions and Google Slides show the fade fallback instead.
